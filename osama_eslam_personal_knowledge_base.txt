SECTION 8: CORE ARCHITECTURAL PATTERNS & ENGINEERING METHODOLOGIES
================================================================================

- Hallucination Control in RAG:
  Osama employs dual-phase guardrails: (1) Contextual Query Rewriting to expand abbreviations and disambiguate references prior to vector search, and (2) Strict Prompt Constraints coupled with fallback policies ("If the context does not explicitly provide the answer, output 'I do not have sufficient information' rather than guessing"). This approach achieved a 91.3% grounded response rate in the DoCrops agricultural assistant.

- Multi-Agent Orchestration:
  Osama utilizes role-based agent architectures (via CrewAI and LangGraph) where distinct agents operate with specialized system prompts, dedicated tools (scrapers, parsers, validators), and Pydantic schemas. Inter-agent communication is structured to prevent unbounded recursion and ensure predictable JSON data interchange.

- Edge AI and Quantization:
  Experienced in taking computationally demanding deep neural networks (e.g., EfficientNet-B3) and applying post-training floating-point quantization (FP16) via TensorFlow Lite to achieve sub-second or near-real-time inference on resource-constrained mobile hardware (Android/Flutter).
